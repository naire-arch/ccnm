# ccnm
comprehensive care and Renal unit nutrition management system for monitoring and evaluation of patient progress under nutrition care with medication and underlying conditions 
[Site:critical Care Unit Nutrition Monitoring
A web application for monitoring and evaluating patient progress in critical care and renal units, covering nutrition care, medication and total parenteral nutrition (TPN) in one place.
Disclaimer: ccnm is a monitoring and evaluation tool only. It is not a diagnostic tool and must not replace clinical judgement or local protocols. Do not enter real patient data in public demo instances.
Live demo: https://naire-arch.github.io/ccnm/

Why this exists
In busy care units, nutrition plans, medication and parenteral feeding are often tracked in separate places. That makes it harder for the care team to see a patient's full progress. ccnm brings these together so dietitians, nurses and doctors can review them side by side.
Features
Nutrition care monitoring: track nutrition plans and progress over time
Medication tracking: record and review medication alongside nutrition
Total parenteral nutrition (TPN): monitor parenteral feeding
Critical care and renal unit support: designed to be usable in both settings
Who it is for
Dietitians, nurses, doctors, and researchers working in critical care and renal settings.
